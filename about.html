About this journal

What this site is

This site is my course journal for IGME 470 — Physical Computing & Alternative Interfaces. Throughout the semester I'll be posting assignments here: research into existing interactive art and devices, write-ups of my own builds, and reflections on what makes an interface feel physical rather than just on-screen.

Each entry is written as a standalone post so it can be linked to directly. This is useful for submitting individual assignments while keeping everything collected in one place.

Feel free to look around, or head back to the full list of posts.
